Verification

How we verify every surgeon

Before any physician touches a patient at Premier, they pass our due diligence protocol.

01

Credential submission

Original medical degree, surgical residency certificate, and hair restoration fellowship documentation.

02

ISHRS verification

Direct confirmation of membership status and any disciplinary history with the International Society of Hair Restoration Surgery.

03

MDCN application

Full CV and credentials submitted to the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria for temporary practice licensure.

04

Observed cases

Minimum 10 supervised procedures at Premier before independent surgical privileges are granted.
