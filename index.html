Built for the C-curve, not the straight shaft.

Afro-textured hair shafts descend in a helical C-curve 3–4mm beneath the scalp. Standard FUE punches assume linear descent, severing the follicle root. Our skin-responsive curved punches and manual angle-adjusting techniques preserve graft integrity.
