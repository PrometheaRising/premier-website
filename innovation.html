The gap

Existing software is trained on straight hair and light skin.

TrichoLAB and other trichoscopy platforms use datasets dominated by European and Asian hair types. They cannot accurately measure coil density, identify C-curve trajectory, or detect early CCCA inflammation in dark skin.

This means Black patients receive misdiagnosed density readings, inappropriate treatment plans, and missed early-warning signs of scarring alopecia.

Technology stack

How CurvTek works

From image capture to surgical recommendation — a fully integrated pipeline.

01

Capture

USB trichoscopy device or smartphone camera captures scalp images under polarised light.

02

Segment

YOLOv11/Mask R-CNN model isolates individual follicles and classifies curl type (3A–4C).

03

Map

Depth and trajectory algorithms predict the subterranean C-curve path for each follicle.

04

Flag

Inflammation markers are compared against CCCA progression baselines. Early warnings issued.

05

Plan

Surgical extraction map generated with optimal punch angle, depth, and torque for each graft zone.

06

Track

Post-op images are compared to baseline. Graft survival and density recovery plotted over 180 days.
